INTELLIGENT TRANSPORT SYSTEMS

Intelligent Transport System

An Intelligent Transport System (ITS) applies electronics, control and communication technology to roads and vehicles, so that traffic operations are managed scientifically and automatically — making the network both more efficient and safer.

There is a limit to how many lanes you can add. ITS is about using the road you already have more intelligently — reading how each vehicle moves, retiming signals, warning of a queue before it forms, and noticing an incident within seconds.

SangLim builds the whole chain: AI deep-learning detection that collects live traffic data, the processing that turns it into usable information, and the roadside displays, bus terminals and control room dashboards that deliver it.

SangLim Tech Vision

From detection on the roadto AI traffic operations,we build it as one flow

The transport infrastructure market has moved past supplying equipment. What matters now is how the data that equipment collects is processed and operated.

SangLim answers that shift with a single chain — detection hardware, display hardware and control-room software, connected as one intelligent transport system.

HOW IT WORKS

From road data to something a driver can read

A sensor reading passes through three stages before it becomes a sentence on a sign.

01ROADSIDE

Collect

Detectors and cameras on the road read every vehicle’s presence, speed, plate and image, second by second.

  • VDS loop / radar detectors
  • AVI plate recognition
  • CCTV video detection
  • GNSS probe data
02CENTRE

Process

AI deep-learning models turn the raw feed into flow states, incident alerts and predicted travel times.

  • Volume & occupancy
  • Automatic incident detection
  • Travel-time prediction
  • Historical archive
03FIELD

Deliver

The processed information goes straight back to the road and to the traveller’s screen.

  • VMS roadside displays
  • BIT bus information
  • Signal control link
  • Open API & apps

C-ITS

Cooperative ITS

Classic ITS sends information down from a control centre. C-ITS lets vehicles, roads and pedestrians exchange it directly — the precondition for automated driving.

Classic ITSC-ITS next generation
DirectionOne way — the centre pushes information downTwo way — vehicles and infrastructure exchange it
SourceRoadside detectors and CCTVInfrastructure plus the moving vehicles themselves
LatencyAfter collection and processingThe moment it happens (hundreds of ms)
CarriersVMS · BIT · signal controlV2V · V2I · V2P · V2N (WAVE / LTE-V2X)

POLICY

Policy direction — ITS Master Plan 2030

Korea’s Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport set out four strategies under the vision of “green, safe and seamless people-centred transport services”.

STRATEGY 01

Infrastructure for new modes of mobility

Laying the road foundation that automated vehicles and UAM will need before they arrive.

STRATEGY 02

Closing the safety blind spots with AI

Video analytics detects pedestrians and two-wheelers that conventional detectors miss.

STRATEGY 03

A base for user-tailored services

Opening the collected traffic data so private services can build on top of it.

STRATEGY 04

Support for exporting Korean ITS

Turning technology proven at home into an export industry.

Source: Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport, “Intelligent Transport Systems Master Plan 2030” (2021) · MOLIT ITS policy materials
